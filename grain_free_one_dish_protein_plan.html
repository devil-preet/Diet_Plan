Calories, training & safety

Starting calorie estimate: 1,850–1,950 kcal/day, assuming the female Mifflin-St Jeor equation, age 24, 81 kg, 162.6 cm and moderate-to-high activity. BMR is about 1,545 kcal; maintenance is estimated around 2,250–2,450 kcal. Actual needs depend on daily movement and training.

Important: This meal template's listed portions may not reach 1,850–1,950 kcal. Protein targets do not guarantee enough total energy. Do not keep cutting food to force fast weight loss; monitor energy, strength, hunger and weekly average weight, and adjust portions with a registered dietitian if needed.

For evening training, a banana 60–120 minutes beforehand is an option if you feel under-fuelled. Grain-free does not mean carbohydrate-free. If performance, recovery, sleep or menstrual health deteriorates, increase food and seek clinical advice. Keep at least one rest/easier day each week.

Use salt to taste in your curd, but keep total salt moderate, especially if you have been advised to restrict sodium. One tablespoon of chia adds fibre; increase gradually and drink enough water. Store cooked eggs and soya promptly in the refrigerator.

How the protein estimate is calculated

Approximate assumptions: eggs 6–6.5 g each; dry soya chunks 50–52 g protein per 100 g; plain curd 3–4 g per 100 g; milk about 3.2 g per 100 ml; chia seeds about 2 g per tablespoon; SUPR serving adjusted to provide 20 g protein. Brands and portions vary. Check the back of your SUPR pouch.

Budget note

This list is designed to be cost-conscious but cannot be guaranteed at ₹200/day without your local prices. Six eggs daily, soya, dairy and chia may exceed the budget in some markets. Compare the cost per 10 g protein and consider reducing costly items or swapping some eggs for soya. The supplement's future replacement cost is separate from current grocery spending.

This is general meal-planning guidance, not a personalized medical prescription. A high-protein diet may need adjustment with kidney disease or other medical conditions. Persistent dizziness, unusual breathlessness, palpitations or marked fatigue during training warrant medical advice.
